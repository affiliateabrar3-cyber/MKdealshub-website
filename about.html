About MKdealshub

Who We Are

MKdealshub is a deal-discovery platform built for Indian online shoppers. We bring together great products across Electronics, Fashion, Home & Kitchen, Beauty, and more — so you don't have to search multiple websites to find good deals.

What We Do

We research and curate products from trusted online stores and present them in one simple, easy-to-browse place. When you click "Check Deal" on any product, you're taken directly to the store's website (such as Amazon or Flipkart) to complete your purchase safely.

Why Trust Us

We only link to well-known, established online stores. We never ask for your payment details, and we don't process any transactions ourselves — all purchases happen securely on the retailer's own website.
